Explanation & Optimality Guarantee:
  1. Safety Objective: The planner integrates both geometric clearance
     (1/d_safe penalty) and per-transition safety (1 - safety) into the
     effective edge weight w(e) = cost + kGeom*(1/d_safe) + kTrans*(1-safety).
  2. Maximizing Safety Distance: Path 1 has a minimum safety clearance of 0.5385,
     while Path 2 provides a minimum safety clearance of 1.5000 (nearly 3x safer).
     Path 2 also features higher transition safety (0.95 vs 0.60).
  3. LPA* Correctness: Because w(e) >= 0 and the heuristic h(s) is scaled
     by cMin = min(w(e)/d(e)), h(s) remains strictly admissible and consistent.
     LPA* is guaranteed to find the globally optimal safe path and preserve all
     incremental replanning guarantees.
